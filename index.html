ATS Parse Inspector

See your resume the way ten parsers read it.

Applicant tracking systems don't look at your layout — they strip it to a text stream and map it into fields. This tool shows where that mapping breaks, per platform, then hands you a clean rebuild. It targets the strictest parser, so it works everywhere. Everything runs in your browser; nothing you paste leaves this page.

Keyword targets come from this posting only — not a generic list.

Parser behavior reflects documented 2026 quirks: Taleo and Workday are the strict extremes, Greenhouse / Lever / Ashby the lenient ones; Workday requires MM/YYYY, header/footer text is dropped across the board, and columns / icons scramble the text stream on legacy parsers.
